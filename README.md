## What I Do

I work with SaaS and product teams to turn complex product challenges into thoughtful, usable, and visually consistent experiences.

My work sits at the intersection of product strategy, UX, UI, brand systems, and team collaboration. I help teams understand what needs to be built, shape the right user experience, and create design systems that make products easier to scale.

```text
Product Design      UX Strategy          UI Systems
Design Systems      Brand Direction      Workshops
SaaS Platforms      Discovery            Interface Quality
```

## How I Help Product Teams

- Design scalable SaaS interfaces from discovery to delivery
- Build and improve design systems, component libraries, and UI foundations
- Facilitate workshops that turn uncertainty into practical product direction
- Audit UX/UI quality and identify friction in complex product flows
- Align brand, product, and interface decisions into one coherent experience
- Partner with founders, product managers, and engineering teams on freelance contracts

## Currently

- Exploring how design systems can make product teams faster without making interfaces feel generic

## A Few Things I Care About

- Interfaces that reduce cognitive load instead of adding decoration
- Systems that help teams ship consistently
- Workshops that create decisions, not just sticky notes
- Design that respects both user needs and business constraints
- Strong product foundations: hierarchy, flows, states, accessibility, and maintainability
